About This Site

This website was created to support a third-grade research project about deer in a forest habitat. It is designed so a young researcher can explore every part of the topic: the animal itself, its home, what it eats, how it behaves, and the survival traits that keep it safe.

What's on This Site

  • Poster Planner — maps directly onto the tri-fold poster assignment (due 12/7), with checklists, title ideas, and illustration tips.
  • Species — the different kinds of deer, plus what kind of animal a deer is (vertebrate, mammal, warm-blooded).
  • Habitat — the forest home: what it looks like, the weather, dangers, where it's found, and bonus facts.
  • Survival Skills — deer adaptations explained in third-grade language, ready for the middle panel paragraph.
  • What They Eat — the seasonal menu and the special four-part stomach.
  • Behavior — herds, daily habits, communication, and migration.
  • Fun Facts — quick facts to share with the class.
  • Safety — staying safe around deer.
  • Sources & Books — websites and books to cite in the project.

Tip: Use the menu at the top of every page to move between topics. Each page links to the others, so you can explore like a real wildlife biologist exploring a forest.
