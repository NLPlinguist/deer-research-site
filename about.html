About This Site

This site was made as a helpful resource for a research project about deer. It is written so that a third grader can read it easily, but it has a lot of detailed facts and many pages so there is plenty to explore.

What This Site Includes

  • Species — meet the different kinds of deer.
  • Habitat — learn where deer live.
  • What They Eat — discover the deer's menu.
  • Behavior — see how deer act every day.
  • Fun Facts — surprising facts to share.
  • Safety — how to stay safe around deer.
  • Sources & Books — trusted sources to cite.

How to Use This Site

Start on the Home page, then follow the links at the top of every page to jump between topics. Each page connects to the others, so you can explore in any order. The pictures come from Wikimedia Commons, a free collection of images.

About the Pictures

The photos on this site are linked from public sources. They are used to help show what deer look like and where they live. If you want to see more pictures, you can visit the image source directly.

Made for Learning

Goal: This site helps a young student learn real facts about deer and use them in a school research project. It is fun to read, easy to navigate, and packed with things to discover.
