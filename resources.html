Sources & Books for Your Project

For a research project, it is important to use reliable sources. Below are websites you can visit and books you can read or buy. You can cite these sources in your project. If you're reading these for a school assignment, remember to ask your teacher how to format your citations.

Websites to Visit

  • National Wildlife Federation — has clear information about deer and wildlife.
  • U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service — reliable facts about deer and other animals.
  • National Geographic — pictures and stories about deer.
  • Wikipedia: White-tailed deer — a good starting point for research.

Note: Wikipedia is a good place to start, but for a school project, double-check facts with a trusted source like a library or a teacher.

Books You Can Read or Buy

  • “Deer: The Animal” — a beginner-friendly book about deer life.
  • “National Geographic Kids: Deer” — has pictures and simple facts perfect for younger readers.
  • Books at your local library — ask a librarian for books about forests, habitats, and wildlife.

Tip: When you cite a source, write down the website or book name, who wrote it, and the date. This makes your project look professional and shows where you found your facts.

More Ways to Research

  • Visit a local park or forest with an adult and look for deer signs.
  • Ask a park ranger or wildlife expert questions.
  • Keep a journal of what you learn each day.
