Science Videos About Deer

These are real, free videos on YouTube from scientists, nature documentary makers, and national parks. Each one is about the science of deer — how they live, how they survive, and how different kinds of deer behave. Click the link to watch it. You can mention in your report that you watched them to learn.

White-Tailed Deer

  • Oh Deer! White-Tailed Deer Biology — A STEM science video explaining white-tailed deer behavior, their life cycle, and how their populations change.
  • Whitetails In America — Full Documentary — A full documentary exploring the remarkable world of white-tailed deer in America.

Elk

  • On the Elk Trail — Full Series | Amazing Yellowstone Migrations — A wildlife biologist uses trail cameras to study elk migrations around Yellowstone. Great for learning how elk move through their habitat.
  • Yellowstone’s Great Migrations | Explorers in the Field — From National Geographic Education. Scientists track elk migrations with satellite collars and explain why elk move between summer and winter ranges.

Moose

  • Moose | Life, Survival, and Adaptations — A wildlife documentary about moose, North America’s tallest mammal, and the adaptations that help them survive in the northern wilderness.

Caribou

  • Being Caribou: Five Months on Foot with an Arctic Herd — A filmmaker follows an Arctic caribou herd for five months on foot. A fascinating look at how caribou live and migrate.
  • The Epic March of the Arctic Caribou — A wildlife documentary about caribou traveling over 1,500 kilometers from the snowy tundra.
  • How Caribou Brave the Arctic Winter — A look at how caribou survive the harsh Arctic winter.

Tip for your report

When you write your poster, you can say something like: “I learned about deer by reading about their habitat and watching science documentaries about white-tailed deer, elk, moose, and caribou.” That shows your teacher you did real research!
