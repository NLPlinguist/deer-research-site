What Do Deer Eat?

Deer are herbivores. That means they eat plants, not animals. Deer do not eat meat. Their whole menu is made up of plants, and the food they eat changes depending on the season. Here is what deer like to munch on.

Leaves and Shrubs

Deer love to eat leaves from trees and bushes. They chew on leaves, twigs, and branches. In the winter, when the ground is covered in snow, leaves and twigs become an important food. Deer will eat the soft inner bark of trees to survive the cold months.

Grass

Grass is a main food for deer, especially elk and moose, which are known for grazing. Grazing means eating grass and plants that grow close to the ground. In summer and fall, green grass gives deer a lot of energy.

Berries, Acorns, and Fruits

Deer also eat sweet things! Berries, acorns, and fruits are favorite snacks. In the fall, acorns from oak trees are a big treat for deer. The sweet food gives them extra energy to prepare for winter.

Seasonal Menu

SeasonWhat Deer Eat
SpringNew leaves, fresh grass, and soft plants
SummerLeaves, grass, and berries
FallAcorns, berries, and leftover leaves
WinterBark, twigs, and whatever food is left under the snow

Fun Food Fact

Did you know? Deer have a very special stomach. It has four sections, and one part helps them break down plants slowly. This lets them get the most nutrition from tough leaves and grass that many animals cannot digest. Scientists call this kind of stomach a rumen.
